Racing Review

Forza Horizon 5

9.4/10

Forza Horizon 5 is one of the easiest racing games to recommend because it understands the fun of driving before it worries about anything else. It drops players into a huge festival-style open world, fills the map with road races, dirt trails, stunt jumps, speed traps, seasonal challenges, and showcase events, then lets you decide what kind of driver you want to be.

The best part is how flexible the game feels. If you want arcade-style chaos, you can throw a supercar across the map at ridiculous speed. If you prefer technical driving, there are tighter routes that reward braking, corner exits, tuning, and clean racing lines. The car list is massive, and because the upgrade system lets you shape vehicles into different classes, even a car that looks ordinary can become one of your favorites with the right setup.

Progression is generous. You earn cars, credits, spins, accolades, and new events constantly, so the game rarely feels like it is holding back the fun. That pace makes it excellent for casual players who only have a short session, but there is enough depth in tuning, rivals, online events, and collection goals for players who want to master it over time.

Best Parts

Open-world freedom, polished driving, huge car variety, gorgeous environments, and a constant stream of things to do.

Watch For

Some event types repeat after long sessions, and players who want strict simulation racing may find it too festival-like.

Verdict

Forza Horizon 5 is a bright, confident, content-heavy racing game that works whether you want a relaxed cruise, a serious tune, a drift session, or a full night of online racing. It earns its high score because nearly every part of it pushes you toward another fun drive.
