RPG Review

Elden Ring

9.7/10

Elden Ring rewards curiosity more than almost any other action RPG. Its world is dangerous, strange, and full of secrets, while its combat gives players loads of ways to build their own style.

Best Parts

Exploration, boss design, build variety, and world atmosphere.

Watch For

Some fights can be very punishing for new players.
