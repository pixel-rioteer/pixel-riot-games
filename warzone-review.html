FPS Review

Call of Duty: Warzone

9.1/10

Warzone is at its best when a squad is communicating, rotating early, and making smart loadout choices. The gunplay is sharp, the match pacing is intense, and every final circle can turn chaotic fast.

Best Parts

Strong weapons, team strategy, and high-pressure endgames.

Watch For

Balance changes can shift the meta quickly.
