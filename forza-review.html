Racing Review

Forza Horizon 5

9.4/10

Forza Horizon 5 is a huge racing playground with bright visuals, loads of cars, and events that make every session feel busy. It is best when you are tuning cars, chasing rivals, and jumping between road races, dirt tracks, and showcases.

Best Parts

Open-world freedom, smooth handling, and great car variety.

Watch For

Some events can feel repetitive after long sessions.
